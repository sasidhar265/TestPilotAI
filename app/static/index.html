Automobile finance coverage guide

Use the quotation catalogue below to scope your requirements. Product availability and eligible combinations depend on your approved configuration.

PCP · Personal Contract Purchase
Instalments, guaranteed future value and optional final payment.
HP · Hire Purchase
Amount financed, instalments, applicable fees and total payable.
LP · Lease Purchase
Instalments, balloon payment and financial reconciliation.
PCH · Personal Contract Hire
Initial and regular rentals, mileage, maintenance and VAT.
BCH · Business Contract Hire
Rental net, VAT and gross values with approved business eligibility.
PFL · Personal Finance Lease
Approved rental structure, maintenance and applicable final rental.
BFL · Business Finance Lease
Finance amount, rentals, residual or balloon and applicable VAT.

Review coverage: vehicle and product eligibility; deposit, term and mileage boundaries; effective-dated rates and campaigns; residual values; maintenance; fees; decimal precision; safe errors and audit evidence.

Supply approved expectations: calculation examples, rounding rules, response mappings and permitted combinations. Product codes alone do not establish regulatory treatment. This reference does not add rules to your requirement.
