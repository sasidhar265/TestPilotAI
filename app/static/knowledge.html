What is saved and reused?

Checking saved knowledge…

Stories and scenarios are saved separately from complete test suites. A suite is saved only after its Quality Gate passes. Repeating a matching request can reuse those saved stages; changing the source, reviewed content, model, rules or standards can require fresh generation.

Look for “Knowledge Agent” reuse events in the activity feed. The suite table’s Uses column counts matching suite lookups. Saved entries are revalidated before reuse.
